A stone inscription in raised South Arabian letters from Ẓafār, the highland capital of the kingdom of Ḥimyar in what is now Yemen. It survives in two joined pieces, four lines on one and five on the other, and the end of the text is broken. It is not explicitly dated; one scholarly estimate places it in the second half of the fifth century CE.

It belongs to the period after the Ḥimyarite kings abandoned the old South Arabian gods, in the late fourth century, for a monotheism whose exact character scholars still debate.
