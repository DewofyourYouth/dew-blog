---

A palimpsest: its parchment was scraped and reused in the ninth or tenth century for a Syriac text, and the earlier writing survives underneath, including Greek and texts in Christian Aramaic of Israel. That dialect was used by Aramaic-speaking Christians in Israel and Transjordan in late antiquity. Its script is close to Syriac Estrangela, which is the font used below.
