after: "The Genesis Apocryphon retells stories from Genesis, many of them in the first person. This column picks up after Lot has separated from Abram, the story of Genesis 13.\n\nAbram, living in the hill country of Bethel, is distressed that Lot has gone. God appears to him in a vision, tells him to climb Ramat-Hazor and look in every direction, and promises the whole land to him and his descendants. Abram then walks the boundaries of the land, from the Gihon to the Euphrates and around to the Red Sea, and returns to settle at the oaks of Mamre. The column then moves on to the war of the four kings against the five, the story of Genesis 14."

One of the first seven scrolls found in Qumran Cave 1 in 1947. It was so badly stuck together that it could not be unrolled for years. It is written in the same square script as modern Hebrew, so the Hebrew letters below are the original letters. It is now in the Shrine of the Book at the Israel Museum.
