---

Hatra, in northern Iraq, was a fortified caravan city on the edge of the Parthian world, from the first to the third century CE. Its temples are full of Aramaic inscriptions in a local script. The photo shows a different Hatran slab, from the eleventh temple and now in the Iraq Museum, to show the script.
