after: "The Mandaean Book of John is a collection of discourses and teachings, many of them put in the mouth of Yahya, John the Baptist, whom the Mandaeans honour. It mixes cosmology, ethics and polemic against other religions, especially Judaism and Christianity. This page belongs to an opening discourse of questions about the order of creation: how wide is the earth, how high is the vault of heaven, and where Adam and Eve came from."
---

A scripture of the Mandaeans, a Gnostic community of southern Iraq and southwestern Iran. Classical Mandaic writes most vowels with letters, which is why the Hebrew-letter column is full of alephs, vavs and yods. The page shown is from Mark Lidzbarski’s edition of the text.
