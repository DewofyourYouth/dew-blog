A cast bronze axe head with a round socket, its blade incised with a short line of Ugaritic alphabetic cuneiform. It comes from the first season of excavation at Ras Shamra, in 1929, as part of a hoard of bronze tools and weapons buried near the temples on Ugarit’s acropolis, several of them inscribed.
