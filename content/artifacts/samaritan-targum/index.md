The Samaritans translated their Torah into their own dialect of Aramaic, written in the Samaritan script, a descendant of the old Hebrew alphabet. No free image of a Targum manuscript was available; the photo shows the Samaritans’ oldest Torah scroll at Nablus, in the same script.
