---

One of three basalt stelae found at Sfire, near Aleppo, recording a treaty between Barga'yah, king of KTK, and Matiʿel, king of Arpad. They are the longest Old Aramaic texts known, and among the earliest treaty texts in a Northwest Semitic language.

As with Zakkur, the original-script lines are shown in Phoenician-block letters.
